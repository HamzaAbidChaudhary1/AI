# Visit the UK as a Standard Visitor

You can usually stay in the UK for up to 6 months as a Standard Visitor.

## Key facts
- Government fee: GBP 127
- Maximum stay: 6 months
- Processing time: 3 weeks
- Biometrics required: yes
- Apply from: VFS Global Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Mirpur
- Proof of funds: You must show you can support yourself and dependants during your trip without working or using public funds.

## Required documents
- A current passport valid for the whole of your stay
- Bank statements for the last 6 months showing you can support yourself
- Evidence of employment or business (employer letter with salary and approved leave)
- Details of where you will stay and your travel plans
- Evidence of ties to your home country (family, job, property)
- If someone else is paying: their bank statements and a letter of support

Visa decisions are made by UK Visas and Immigration. Do not use anyone who says they can guarantee a visa.
