# Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment

Legal overseas employment from Pakistan goes only through Overseas Employment Promoters (OEPs) licensed by BEOE, or through the Overseas Employment Corporation.
Promoters may not charge more than the service charges notified by the Government, and every payment must be against a proper receipt.
Visit or tourist visas are not work visas. Anyone selling a "work visa" on a visit visa is breaking the law.

## How to verify a promoter
- Check the licence number on the BEOE website
- Visit the Protector of Emigrants office in your city
- Never pay cash without a receipt
